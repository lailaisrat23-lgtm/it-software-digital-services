Privacy notice

This website is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, so visiting it does not create a profile of you.

If you email us or complete the enquiry form, we receive the details you chose to send: your name, email address, optional company and phone number, your partner type and the description of the work. We use them to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to run the engagement.

  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not add you to a marketing list without a separate opt-in.
  • Enquiries are kept for 24 months and then deleted, unless a contract requires longer retention.
  • Partner and end-client material is only shared after an NDA is signed by both parties.
  • You can ask for a copy of what we hold, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@techspire.dev.

Terms of business

Engagements run under a written statement of work signed by both parties. The summary below describes the standard partner position; the signed document always takes precedence.

  • Fixed-scope phases are invoiced 40% at kick-off, 40% at the mid-point demo and 20% on acceptance.
  • Rolling pods are invoiced monthly in advance, with two months' notice to end the arrangement.
  • Intellectual property in delivered work transfers to the partner on final payment.
  • We do not approach a partner's end client directly, and we decline their inbound work for 24 months.
  • Every project includes a 30-day defect warranty; new features are new scope.
  • Liability is limited to the fees paid in the preceding twelve months.

Accessibility statement

This site targets WCAG 2.2 level AA. It uses semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus states, and controls that work from the keyboard. Carousels pause on hover and focus, and animation is disabled when your system asks for reduced motion.

If something here is difficult to use — a colour contrast problem, a control that will not respond to the keyboard, a screen reader announcement that makes no sense — tell us and we will fix it. Email hello@techspire.dev.
