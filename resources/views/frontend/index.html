    How we work

    A delivery process you can audit

    No black boxes and no status decks written the night before. You see the same board, repository and test results we do.

    1. Discovery

      Interview the people who will use it, audit what exists, agree what success means in numbers.

    2. Embed

      Your repo, your ceremonies, your definition of done. A named lead owns the relationship.

    3. Build in increments

      Two-week cycles, a working demo every fortnight, and a production deploy from week one.

    4. Hand back

      Documentation, tests and a written handover your client's team can run without us.
