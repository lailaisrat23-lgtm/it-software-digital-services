Engagement models

Three ways to work together

Pick the shape that matches how much is still unknown. We will say so if we think you have chosen the wrong one.

A defined outcome, a fixed price

Best when requirements are well understood and the main risk is delivery speed. We scope the work in phases, quote each phase, and hold the price unless you change the scope in writing.

  • Written scope and acceptance criteria
  • Fortnightly demos against the plan
  • Change requests priced before work starts
  • 30-day defect warranty after launch

A stable pod on your roadmap

Best for products that keep evolving. A named group of designers and engineers works to your priorities on a monthly retainer, with the same people turning up each sprint.

  • Named team with a delivery lead
  • Shared backlog and definition of done
  • Monthly reporting on throughput
  • Two months' notice, no lock-in

One to three weeks to decide

Best when the opportunity is real but the shape is unclear. We interview users, audit the current systems, and give you a plan you can execute with or without us.

  • Stakeholder and user interviews
  • Technical and data audit
  • Prototype of the riskiest workflow
  • Fee credited if delivery follows
