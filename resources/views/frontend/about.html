Our story

Started by fixing one warehouse system

Techspire began in 2011 with three engineers and a warehouse near Bristol whose stock system had drifted so far from reality that staff kept a paper backup. We rewrote it, then spent the next two years watching people use it every day — which taught us more about software than any framework since.

That pattern still defines the studio. We are more interested in what happens at the loading bay than in what happens on a slide. Most of our work now is web platforms, mobile tools for field teams, and the data plumbing that makes both trustworthy.

In 2016 a software house asked us to take over a project they had sold but could not staff. That single engagement became the model for the whole business: we deliver, they keep the client, and neither side has to pretend the arrangement is something else.

How we behave

Four rules we actually enforce

These are the things we talk about in reviews and retrospectives. They are not posters in a reception area.

  • Ship something real

    A working slice in production beats a perfect specification. Every engagement deploys early, even if the audience is small.

  • Write it down

    Decisions live in the repository, not in someone's memory. If the reasoning is not recorded, it will be re-argued in six months.

  • Say no early

    If a request will create a maintenance burden nobody can staff, we say so before the estimate rather than after the invoice.

  • Leave it maintainable

    Handover is part of the deliverable. If your team cannot run it without us, we have not finished the job.

Security & confidentiality

Your client's material stays yours

Most partners bring us in after their own client has already asked the awkward questions. These are the answers we give in writing, before anyone is granted repository access.

Contracts before code

An NDA and a subcontractor agreement are signed before we see anything. We also accept flow-down clauses and partner-specific security policies.

IP assigned on payment

Copyright in delivered work transfers to you on final payment, and every engineer signs an assignment waiver. Nothing we build carries a claim on your client.

Named, least-privilege access

Every account is a named engineer, never a shared login. Secrets stay in your vault, not in our repository, and access is revoked within one day of an engagement ending.

Screened people, managed devices

Background checks are run where your contract requires them. Client material never touches an unmanaged machine: encrypted disks, enforced screen locks, no local copies.

Data kept where you need it

Staging and production default to EU regions. Personal data is minimised, test fixtures are synthetic, and anything we hold is deleted on request with written confirmation.

A defined incident path

Suspected exposure is reported to your named lead within 24 hours, with one engineer owning it until it closes and a written post-mortem afterwards.
