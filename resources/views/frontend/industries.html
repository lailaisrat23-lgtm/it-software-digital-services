Protections 01–04

  1. Your client stays yours

    We never approach a partner's client directly, and we decline inbound work from them for 24 months.

  2. No surprise invoices

    Anything that changes budget is written down and approved before work starts.

  3. Named people, not a pool

    The engineers you meet in discovery are the ones who deliver. No bait-and-switch after signature.

  4. White-label quality

    Code, docs and presentations carry your branding. Nothing we produce contradicts your standards.

The full agreement, NDA and data processing terms are sent before any client material is shared.
